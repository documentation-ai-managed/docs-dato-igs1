## Overview

dato_igs1 provides a centralized space to create, organize, and collaborate on your project's documentation. You build knowledge bases, API references, and guides with an intuitive editor supporting MDX, version control, and search. Teams use it to streamline onboarding, reduce support tickets, and maintain up-to-date resources.

<Callout kind="info">
  Get started in minutes—no complex setup required. Connect your GitHub repo for automatic syncs.
</Callout>

## Quick Start

Follow these steps to set up your first documentation space.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Account" icon="user-plus">
    Sign up at `https://dashboard.dato_igs1.com/register`. Verify your email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="New Project" icon="plus">
    Click "New Space" and connect your GitHub repo or start blank.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add First Doc" icon="file-plus">
    Create `introduction.mdx` and use the MDX editor.

    <CodeGroup tabs="MDX Example">
      ```mdx
      ## Hello World

      Welcome to your docs!

      <Callout kind="success">
        Your first doc is live.
      </Callout>
      ```
    </CodeGroup>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish" icon="globe">
    Hit "Publish" to generate your site at `https://docs.dato_igs1.com/your-space`.
  </Step>
</Steps>
