description: Quick guide to start with dato_igs1 projects.
---

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Login to dato_igs1">
    Go to `https://app.datoigs1.com` and login with your credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new project">
    Click **New Project** on the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill details">
    Enter your project name and (optionally) a description. Click **Create**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Begin editing">
    Select your new project to add docs, create folders, and organize content.
  </Step>
</Steps>
