description: Resolve missing project after creating in dato_igs1.
---

## Problem

- You created a project, but it doesn't show up on the dashboard.

## Possible Causes

- Page not refreshed
- Network delay
- Account sync issue

## Solution

<Steps>
  <Step title="Refresh dashboard">
    Click the refresh icon or reload your browser tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log out and back in">
    To ensure account is updated, log out, then log in again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact support">
    If the project is still missing, email support@datoigs1.com.
  </Step>
</Steps>
