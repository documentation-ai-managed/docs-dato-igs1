## Quick Start

To create a new documentation project:

1. Log in to your dato_igs1 account.
2. Click the **New Project** button on your dashboard.
3. Enter a name and optional description.
4. Click **Create** to finish.

The new project will appear in your projects list. Open it to add and organize docs right away.

## Tips

- Projects can be renamed anytime.
- Use folders to organize your docs files.
